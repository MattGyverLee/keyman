## What the IDE does with a project group (found on the way)

1. **Projects are listed only if the group has the per-project `<Target>`s.** A group file with
   only `<Projects>` items opens with no projects.
2. **Projects are matched to targets by name.** With index suffixes on the target names, nothing
   loaded. Two projects with the same name (`windows/src/desktop/setup/setup.dproj` and
   `developer/src/setup/setup.dproj`) threw the matching off: the second `setup` and the last
   project in the group (TIKE) were silently dropped. The driver now leaves the second same-named
   project out, so it falls back to its prompt.
3. **Dependencies are built for every platform being built.** With each project depending on the
   one before it, building tsysinfox64 (Win64) pulled every Win32-only tool into a Win64 build,
   and devtools failed (`F2613 Unit 'Registry' not found`). With no dependencies, each build-group
   row's own Platforms and Enabled are honoured, and rows are built in listed order (= CI order).
4. **The `Active` attribute of a `<BuildGroup>` is ignored on open**; pick the group in the
   dropdown.
5. **The IDE does not notice that the group file was rewritten** while it is open; close and
   reopen it.
6. **Duplicate ProjectGuid stops loading** with a "Duplicate GUID Found" dialog that offers to
   rewrite a `.dproj`. `developer/src/kmdbrowserhost` and `developer/src/setup` were copies of
   the Desktop projects with the same GUIDs; they got new GUIDs in #16044 (the GUID is used only
   by the IDE). The driver refuses to write a group with duplicate GUIDs.
